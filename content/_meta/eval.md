## Questions

1. What is communicative anosmia and what narrative strategies do perfume commercials use to compensate for it?

2. What racial and gender norms do mainstream Western perfume commercials reproduce, and what is the mechanism by which they achieve the status of "natural"?

3. How do palimpsestic narrative structures work in perfume advertising, and what cultural sources do they draw on?

4. How does K-Pop's treatment of masculinity (particularly the "flower boy" aesthetic) differ from conventions in Western fragrance commercials, and what accounts for the difference?

5. What distinguishes Korean perfume market strategies from Western ones, particularly around scent and memory?

6. How do parasocial fan-idol relationships in K-Pop extend the celebrity-endorsement logic of perfume advertising?

7. What role does bodily movement play in perfume commercials as a surrogate for the scent being advertised?

8. How do the theoretical frameworks of Barthes (myth), Hall (representation), and Butler (performativity) apply to the analysis of gender in perfume commercials?
