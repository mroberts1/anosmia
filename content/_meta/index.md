- Kenzo World as a test case: does aesthetic subversion of gender norms require ideological critique to count as subversion?

- Baudelaire's "Correspondences" as the ur-text of perfume advertising aesthetics: from symbolist synesthesia to the contemporary short-film commercial.
- The "Perfume" MV's white female model as recipient: write on what this composition encodes about desire, aspiration, and crossover marketing.
- The gap between sold aspiration and actual product is publicly acknowledged and yet it works. What does this say about advertising persuasion?
